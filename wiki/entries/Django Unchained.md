## Overview

**Django Unchained** is a 2012 American revisionist Western film written and directed by **Quentin Tarantino**. Set in the antebellum American South, the film blends spaghetti western and blaxploitation elements to deliver a brutal but stylized critique of slavery, violence, and vengeance.

- **Director**: Quentin Tarantino  
- **Starring**: Jamie Foxx, Christoph Waltz, Leonardo DiCaprio, Kerry Washington, Samuel L. Jackson  
- **Genre**: Western / Drama / Action  
- **Runtime**: 165 minutes

## Plot Summary

Django, a slave in the American South, is freed by bounty hunter **Dr. King Schultz**, who needs him to identify a group of outlaws. After proving his skill, Django partners with Schultz in a quest to find and rescue his wife, **Broomhilda**, from the sadistic plantation owner **Calvin Candie**.

Their journey leads them deep into the brutal and hypocritical world of Southern slavery, culminating in explosive violence and retribution.
